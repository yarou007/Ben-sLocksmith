Offices & medical buildings

Keep business access organized when staff or tenants change

Coordinate keys, commercial locks and door-side access hardware around the people who need access.

  • Rekey commercial locks after staff or tenant turnover
  • Plan master-key access by door and authorized role
  • Review door hardware with your access-control integrator
  • Identify staff entrances, shared doors and access windows
Do all locks need replacing after staff turnover?

A rekey may change which keys operate a compatible lock without replacing the entire lock. The condition, key system and access requirements determine the right approach.

Can I include existing access control?

Yes. Include the hardware model and integrator contact. Describe the door-side problem and any reader, programming or system work you need coordinated.

Fire doors & exit hardware

A door that will not close, latch or release needs attention

Turn a failed inspection item or exit-hardware problem into a clear repair or inspection request.

  • Panic bars and exit devices that stick or fail to relatch
  • Door closers that leak, slam or leave a door open
  • Fire-door repair requests tied to specific report items
  • Agree the repair, inspection and documentation scope
What if a fire door failed inspection?

Share the report item, door identifier and photos of the hardware and labels. We will discuss the requested correction and any inspection or documentation requirements.

Does a repair provide final compliance approval?

Repair and inspection are separate scopes. The responsible inspector or authority determines final acceptance for the opening. Include their requirements when requesting work.
